# Data Analytics Portfolio

Data analytics projects completed as part of my coursework at Carnegie Mellon University, focused on relational databases, SQL, data visualization, data preparation, and NoSQL analysis.

## Projects

### [BikeShare & Pennsylvania Demographics Analysis](bikeshare-demographics-analysis)

**Tools:** SQL, MySQL, Valentina Studio, Tableau

Analyzed BikeShare usage across four U.S. metro areas and Pennsylvania demographic trends using multi-table SQL queries, CTEs, joins, CASE logic, aggregation, and visualization.

**Key findings:**
- Estimated BikeShare commute share ranged from 13.4% in Portland to 24.8% in Los Angeles.
- Dense Pennsylvania counties grew by an average of 4.0%, compared with declines of 1.4% for moderate-density counties and 5.0% for sparse counties.

### [NBA Three-Point Scoring Analysis](nba-three-point-analysis)

**Tools:** MongoDB, Studio 3T

Used MongoDB aggregation pipelines to analyze changes in the role of three-point shooting in NBA scoring between the 1990s and 2000s.

**Key finding:**
- Three-point field goals increased from approximately 12.08% of total scoring in the 1990s to 17.45% in the 2000s, an increase of roughly 45%.
